# Cards and roast tone

Your credit cards (names only, never numbers), so every expense can be tied to the bill it lands on. Plus how blunt you want the coach to be.

## What onboarding asks
1. **Which cards do you use?** Names only, like "Chase Sapphire" or "Amex Gold". **Never enter card numbers.**
2. **Default card per category**, so new expenses pre-fill.
3. **Roast tone:**
   - **gentle:** encouraging, no jabs.
   - **honest:** plain numbers, no sugar-coating. This is the default.
   - **savage:** you asked for it. Applies to categories you said you'd cut; protected categories always get the gentle voice.

## Common mistakes
- Not picking a card for an expense. The card summary then can't tell you which bill is coming.
- Carrying a balance. Card interest wipes out everything else in the plan; pay statements in full.

**Writes:** `cards[]`, `categories[].card`, `tone`
